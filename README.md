# PeopleSense Workforce Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

PeopleSense Workforce Analytics Dashboard is an enterprise workforce intelligence solution developed using Power BI to provide leadership teams with a comprehensive view of workforce trends, talent movement, compensation metrics, and organizational performance indicators.

The solution enables business leaders and HR stakeholders to monitor workforce health, identify trends, analyze employee movements, and make data-driven workforce planning decisions through interactive dashboards and KPI scorecards.

---

## Business Problem

Organizations often maintain workforce data across multiple systems, making it difficult to obtain a consolidated view of employees, hiring activities, attrition trends, compensation metrics, and organizational performance.

The objective of this project was to create a centralized analytics platform that transforms workforce data into actionable insights for business leaders and decision-makers.
